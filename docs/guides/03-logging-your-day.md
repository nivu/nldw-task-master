# Logging your day

The **Time** page records what you worked on each day. It is designed to take
under a minute, at the end of the day, on a phone.

## Adding a line

1. The page opens on today. Use the arrows to move to another day.
2. Under **Projects**, tap the project you worked on. Projects you are
   allocated to are listed; you may also log against a project you are not
   allocated to, and it is simply marked *not allocated*.
3. Under **Not for a project**, tap **Learning**, **Internal work**, **Admin**
   or **Other** for time that was not client work.
4. For each line, write **What did you do?** — one or two sentences. This is
   the only record of the work itself, so make it something a colleague would
   understand in a month's time.
5. Enter hours in the **Office** and **Home** boxes. The plus and minus buttons
   step by half an hour; you can also type a number.
6. Tap **Save day**. The whole day saves at once.

## Rules that will stop you

- A line is for **either** a project **or** an activity, never both.
- The same project or activity cannot appear twice on one day. Combine the
  lines.
- A day has an upper limit on total hours. The portal tells you what it is.

## Editing a day

A day stays editable until the end of its week plus a week's grace. After that
it is locked, and the page tells you when that happens. Everything you log
before the lock can be changed freely.

## Working on a leave day

If you log hours on a day you booked as leave, the portal warns you but lets
you save. People do finish something on a sick day, and refusing would make the
effort vanish from the project.

## Why the note matters

There is no task list in the portal on purpose. Task tracking lives in the
tools your project already uses. The note is what turns "6 hours on Project X"
into something a manager can defend in a budget conversation.
