# Booking leave and work from home

Everything happens on the **Calendar**. Tap a day, choose what it is, confirm.

## The three categories

| Category | When you can mark it | Needs approval |
|---|---|---|
| **Work from home** | Today or any future day | Yes |
| **Casual leave** | Future days only — it must be requested before the day itself | Yes |
| **Sick leave** | Today only — nobody knows they will be ill next Tuesday | Yes |

If you are unwell today, mark it as sick leave. If you try to mark casual leave
for today, the portal will tell you to use sick leave instead.

## Booking a day

1. Tap the day on the calendar. Weekends, declared holidays and days that have
   already passed cannot be selected.
2. Choose the category and whether it is a **full day** or a **half day**.
3. Add a reason if you want to. Your lead sees it when deciding; the rest of
   the team never does. The day-by-day team list shows only the category.
4. Confirm. The day shows as **pending** until your lead decides.

The portal shows your remaining balance for that category before you confirm,
and will not let a balance go below zero.

## Changing your mind

You can withdraw a booking any time up to the end of the day it applies to.
After that the day is **locked**: what happened, happened. If a locked day is
wrong, an admin can correct it, and the correction is recorded as an admin
action.

## What "pending" and "approved" mean

- **Pending** — waiting for your lead. It already counts against your balance,
  so you cannot book the same days twice.
- **Approved** — confirmed.
- **Rejected** — your lead said no and wrote why. The days go back to your
  balance.
- **Withdrawn** — you cancelled it. The days go back.
- **Released** — the day became a company holiday after you booked it. The days
  go back, and you are told.

A request nobody has decided by the end of the day it applies to is approved
automatically overnight, so an absence is never left in limbo.

## Balances

Each category has a monthly allowance set by an admin. The calendar shows, per
category, the allowance for the month, what you have used, and what remains.
Unused days carry forward according to the company's policy, which admins can
see under **Admin → Policy**. Your yearly history is under **Account**.

## Holidays

Company holidays are marked on the calendar and cannot be booked. They consume
nobody's allowance.
