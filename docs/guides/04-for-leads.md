# For leads

A lead approves leave for the people whose approver they are. If you were made
somebody's approver under **Admin → People**, their requests come to you.

## Team

**Team** shows who is present, working from home, on leave or unaccounted for
on a given day. It defaults to today and can be moved to any date.

It shows the **category only**. A reason somebody wrote on a sick day is
health information about a named colleague, and it does not appear on a screen
that is glanced at across a room.

Below the roster is each person's balance and usage for the month.

## Approvals

**Approvals** lists every request waiting for you, with the reason the person
wrote, because you cannot reasonably decide a request whose reason you cannot
read.

- **Approve** confirms it.
- **Reject** needs a note. The button stays disabled until you have written
  one. The person sees your note.

A request you have not decided by the end of the day it applies to is approved
automatically overnight.

## Flagging an absence nobody booked

If someone is away and nothing was booked, use **Mark absent** next to their
name on the team view for that day. It records the absence so the day is not silently
counted as present. It does not consume the person's allowance, and they see
it on their calendar.

## Effort

**Effort** shows where your reports' hours are going: per project, what people
worked on in the last week, the capacity forecast from allocations, and
**coverage** — how much of the timesheet actually exists.

Read the coverage banner before quoting any total. Hours computed over a
half-filled timesheet are not roughly right; they are lower than reality.

Leads see hours, never money.
