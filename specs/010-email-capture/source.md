# Source brief — automatic email capture

The owner's words, 19 October 2026, kept verbatim. `spec.md` wins where they
disagree.

> See, I can't be assigning sales and leads labels manually; that is quite
> tedious and I might even miss some.
>
> Basically, what we can do is every hour, we can look into the Nunnari Labs
> email ID—the primary emails alone. Even if the sales or leads emails landed
> in other tabs like updates or promotions, I can move them to primary. So,
> every hour we just do a complete sweep of the primary email inbox and then
> we auto-label them as a lead or a deal.
>
> Anything that has an SOW attached we can consider as a deal, and anything
> that is in the initial discussion phase we can consider as a lead, right? So
> you can classify it.
>
> To start with, we can do a complete sweep of the last six months, starting
> this financial year from April 2026 until today, 19 October 2026. This could
> be done in one shot; we can also auto-label them. I mean, after our internal
> AI-based classification, we can label them. We can also store which emails
> have already been parsed, interpreted, and logged into our system so that
> we can do it one time. For the next iteration, any new incoming email could
> be read and then analyzed, and only sales and deals-related items could be
> populated in the CRM.
>
> This way, I think it would be more efficient. What do you think?
